Development and Evaluation of NoduleCare: A YOLOv8-Based System for Detecting Pulmonary Nodules on Thoracic CT Scans

Applied computer vision research project targeting computer-aided pulmonary screening. NoduleCare utilizes a fine-tuned YOLOv8 convolutional architecture trained across accelerated cloud GPU runtime environments to accurately localize and classify early-stage solitary pulmonary nodules within axial slice thoracic Computed Tomography (CT) scans.
